Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of eleven players.

The main formats of international cricket are Test cricket, One Day International (ODI), and Twenty20 International (T20I).

In Test cricket, a match can last up to five days and each team normally has two innings.

An ODI match consists of 50 overs per side.

A T20I match consists of 20 overs per side.

A cricket team has eleven players on the field.

The batting team tries to score runs, while the bowling and fielding team tries to dismiss the batters and restrict the number of runs.

A boundary scored by hitting the ball to the boundary without bouncing is worth six runs.

A boundary where the ball reaches the boundary after bouncing is worth four runs.

A wicket is the dismissal of a batter.

Common ways of dismissal include bowled, caught, leg before wicket (LBW), run out, stumped, and hit wicket.

The International Cricket Council (ICC) is the global governing body for international cricket.
